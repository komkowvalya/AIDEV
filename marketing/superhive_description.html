As an automotive CG artist, I always needed a simple setup for generating specific license plate numbers. A displacement map with a PSD preset was my first solution, but it had limits. So here is a node preset that builds the actual geometry of a license plate for any US state, with any number.

It is super easy to make your own plate, even if you have never opened Blender before.

What you get

  • All 50 states + Washington DC: 51 ready presets with artwork, slogans, fonts and colors
  • Any number: real embossed letters, or flat printed with one value
  • State symbols as separators: Florida orange, Georgia peach, PA keystone, NM Zia
  • Customize everything: number, colors, background image and fonts in a few clicks
  • Easy bend: wrap the plate around any bumper with one slider
  • Plastic frame with your own dealer text, plus optional chrome screws
  • Imperfections in one click: road dirt, mud speckles and squashed bugs

Made for production

  • Asset manager friendly: just drag & drop
  • Photorealistic even in EEVEE, and works in both EEVEE and CYCLES
  • Export-friendly to any software, separated by materials
  • Great for naming design versions of a car model

Under the hood

One clean modifier panel. US plate specs (12" × 6", 7" hole spacing) are built in, so you only see the inputs you need.

Important notes

  • The license is okay for commercial use. However, it doesn't allow you to sell produced assets with the addon.
  • The file includes various fonts chosen to fit each state's standard. Fonts are not part of the sale and may need additional licensing, depending on your usage.
